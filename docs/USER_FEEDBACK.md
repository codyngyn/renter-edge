# Renter feedback protocol

Status: not conducted. No participant outcomes or demand claims are established.

Ask 3–5 consenting renters to inspect a listing and identify its known costs and unresolved questions, then repeat with the report. Use different comparable listings and vary the order to reduce learning effects. Record task completion time, incorrectly assumed costs, questions identified, and whether source labels were understood. Ask what they would still use a broker or general chat for. Do not collect addresses, finances, or other unnecessary personal information.

Success criteria to evaluate, not claimed results: fewer unsupported cost assumptions and clear understanding that supported does not mean independently verified. Record negative findings as well as positive ones. The owner must recruit participants; this repository does not send outreach automatically.
